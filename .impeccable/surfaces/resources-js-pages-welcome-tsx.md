## Direction contract

OWN-WORLD: Ground is the existing pale mesh with brand-blue wash; the RAB is one large frosted glass sheet with hairline ruled rows, a column header row and a total row. Ink #17191D, paper #E8EBED, blue #244CFF only as the live/active mark. Display in Bricolage Grotesque, body and numerals in Onest with tabular figures. Row state is a mark, not a hue: struck check = vendor locked, blue ring = offers to compare, dash = not started. Share column is a thin ruled bar per row in the budget-split hues.
